# Blocked Access Policy

When a source is paywalled, unreachable, or otherwise unavailable:

1. **Cite from search metadata** — record what the search returned (title, author, date, venue, snippet) and mark the source `blocked`.
2. **Never guess at contents** — do not describe what a blocked source says, quote it, or paraphrase claims from it.
3. **Mark status honestly** — use `blocked` in the evidence ledger and provenance sidecar.
4. **Search for alternatives** — look for accessible versions (preprints, open-access mirrors, institutional repositories) before giving up.
5. **Note the gap** — in the `## What we did not find` section, record that a relevant source was identified but could not be accessed.

A `blocked` source may appear in a search result but must never be load-bearing
support for a claim.
