# ASME Standards (American Society of Mechanical Engineers)

## Scope

ASME publishes standards for mechanical engineering: pressure vessels,
piping, boilers, elevators, nuclear components, and dimensional standards.
The most commonly referenced are ASME B31 (pressure piping) and ASME BPVC
(Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code). These are legally mandated for pressure
equipment in the US and Canada.

## Key standards

| Standard | Title | Access | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| ASME BPVC | Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code | Paywalled | Design, fabrication, inspection of boilers and pressure vessels (10+ sections) |
| ASME B31.1 | Power Piping | Paywalled | Piping for electric power plants, geothermal, district heating |
| ASME B31.3 | Process Piping | Paywalled | Piping for petroleum refineries, chemical, pharmaceutical, textile, paper, semiconductor, cryogenic |
| ASME B31.4 | Pipeline Transportation Systems for Liquids and Slurries | Paywalled | Liquid hydrocarbon and other liquid pipelines |
| ASME B31.5 | Refrigeration Piping | Paywalled | Refrigeration and heat transfer components |
| ASME B31.8 | Gas Transmission and Distribution Piping | Paywalled | Gas pipelines and distribution |
| ASME B31.9 | Building Services Piping | Paywalled | Piping in commercial/institutional buildings |
| ASME B16 | Valves, Flanges, Fittings, Gaskets | Paywalled | Dimensional and pressure-temperature ratings for piping components |
| ASME Y14.5 | Dimensioning and Tolerancing (GD&T) | Paywalled | Geometric dimensioning and tolerancing — the US GD&T standard |
| ASME B46.1 | Surface Texture | Paywalled | Surface roughness, waviness, lay |
| ASME A17.1 | Safety Code for Elevators and Escalators | Paywalled | Elevator and escalator design, construction, operation |

## Access

- **Paywalled:** All ASME standards require purchase or ASME subscription.
- **No public API.**
- **Free preview:** ASME provides table of contents and selected pages.
- **Citation fallback:** Cite by standard + section + edition.

## ASME BPVC — the central pressure vessel code

The Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code is divided into sections:

| Section | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| I | Power Boilers | Design, fabrication, inspection of power boilers |
| II | Materials | Material specifications (Part A: ferrous, Part B: nonferrous, Part C: welding consumables, Part D: properties) |
| III | Nuclear Facility Components | Nuclear power plant components (Classes 1, 2, 3) |
| IV | Heating Boilers | Design, fabrication of heating boilers |
| V | Nondestructive Examination | NDE methods and personnel qualification |
| VIII | Pressure Vessels | Div. 1 (design by rule), Div. 2 (design by analysis), Div. 3 (high pressure) |
| IX | Welding, Brazing, and Fusing Qualifications | Welder and procedure qualifications |
| X | Fiber-Reinforced Plastic Pressure Vessels | FRP vessel design and fabrication |
| XI | Rules for Inservice Inspection of Nuclear Power Plant Components | In-service inspection |
| XII | Rules for Construction and Continued Service of Tanks | Transport tanks |

For pressure vessel research, Section VIII Division 1 (design by rule) is
the most commonly referenced.

## ASME B31 — pressure piping codes

The B31 series covers different piping service types. Do not mix them:

| Code | Service | Key difference |
|---|---|---|
| B31.1 | Power piping | High temperature, power plants |
| B31.3 | Process piping | Chemical/petrochemical — most common for industrial |
| B31.4 | Liquid pipelines | Long-distance liquid transport |
| B31.8 | Gas pipelines | Gas transmission/distribution |
| B31.9 | Building services | Lower pressure, building systems |

B31.3 (Process Piping) is the most commonly referenced for chemical and
petrochemical facility design.

## Mandatory-language conventions

- **"shall"** — mandatory
- **"should"** — recommended
- **"may"** — permitted
- **Mandatory appendix** — enforceable
- **Nonmandatory appendix** — informative only

## Known conflicts and cross-checks

| Conflicts with | Issue |
|---|---|
| API standards | American Petroleum Institute standards (API 650 for tanks, API 620 for low-pressure storage, API 579 for fitness-for-service) overlap with ASME for petroleum facilities. Project specification designates which governs. |
| ASTM | Material specifications. ASME BPVC Section II references ASTM material specs. |
| ASCE 7 | Loads on mechanical equipment. ASME equipment design uses ASCE 7 loads. |
| NFPA | Fire protection for mechanical systems. NFPA may govern fire protection where ASME governs pressure. |

## Jurisdiction notes

- **US:** ASME BPVC is adopted by reference in all 50 states for boiler and
  pressure vessel safety. Compliance is legally mandated.
- **Canada:** CSA B51 (Boiler, Pressure Vessel, and Pressure Piping Code)
  adopts ASME with Canadian additions.
- **International:** EN 13445 (European Pressure Vessel Code) is the European
  equivalent. Not interchangeable with ASME BPVC.
- **CRN (Canadian Registration Number):** Pressure equipment in Canada requires
  provincial registration in addition to ASME stamp.

## When to use

Use this reference when the research question involves:
- Pressure vessel design or inspection (ASME BPVC Section VIII)
- Process piping design (ASME B31.3)
- Power piping (ASME B31.1)
- Gas or liquid pipelines (ASME B31.4/8)
- Geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (ASME Y14.5)
- Elevator safety (ASME A17.1)

Do NOT use for: structural steel (use AISC 360), structural loads (use ASCE 7),
or electrical systems (use NEC/IEEE).
