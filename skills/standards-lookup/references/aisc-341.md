# AISC 341 — Seismic Provisions for Structural Steel Buildings

## Scope

Seismic design and detailing requirements for steel structures in high-seismic
regions. Supplements AISC 360 — where the two overlap, AISC 341 governs.
Applies to Special Moment Frames (SMF), Special Concentrically Braced Frames
(SCBF), Eccentrically Braced Frames (EBF), Buckling-Restrained Braced Frames
(BRBF), and Special Plate Shear Walls (SPSW). Mandatory in the US for
Seismic Design Categories D, E, and F.

## Relationship to AISC 360

- **AISC 360** = gravity + wind + general steel design.
- **AISC 341** = seismic-specific requirements that *supplement or override*
  AISC 360 for seismic design.
- When both apply, **AISC 341 governs** for seismic limit states.
- AISC 341 also references **AISC 358** for prequalified connections.

## When to use

Use this reference when:
- The structure is in SDC D, E, or F (per ASCE 7).
- The system is SMF, SCBF, EBF, BRBF, or SPSW.
- The question involves strong-column/weak-beam, panel zone, or link beam
  design.
- The question involves BRB qualification or seismic connection detailing.
