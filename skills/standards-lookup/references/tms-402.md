# TMS 402/602 — Building Code for Masonry Structures / Specification for Masonry Structures

## Scope

The US standards for structural masonry design (TMS 402) and construction
(TMS 602). Covers reinforced and unreinforced masonry walls, columns,
pilasters, beams (lintels), and connections. Applies to clay brick,
concrete block (CMU), and stone masonry. Referenced by the IBC for masonry
structures.

## Key concepts unique to masonry

- **Net vs gross area:** Masonry design uses net mortar-bedded area, not gross
  area. This affects all stress calculations.
- **Grout vs no-grout:** Reinforced masonry may be fully grouted, partially
  grouted, or ungrouted — each has different design rules.
- **Running bond vs other bonds:** Running bond is the default; other bonds
  (stack bond) have reduced allowable stresses.
- **Type N / S / M mortar:** Mortar type affects allowable stress and bond
  strength. Type S is most common for structural masonry.

## When to use

Use this reference when:
- Designing reinforced or unreinforced masonry walls.
- Designing masonry columns, pilasters, or lintels.
- Specifying mortar type or grout for structural masonry.
- Checking masonry anchorage or connections.
- Designing masonry veneer.
