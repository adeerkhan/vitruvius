# NFPA Standards (National Fire Protection Association)

## Scope

NFPA publishes fire protection, life safety, and electrical standards. The
most commonly referenced are NFPA 70 (NEC — see the dedicated NEC reference
file), NFPA 101 (Life Safety Code), and NFPA 13 (Sprinkler Systems). NFPA
standards are freely available to *view* via the NFPA website
(registration required) but not to download or quote at length.

## Key standards

| Standard | Title | Free view? | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| NFPA 70 (NEC) | National Electrical Code | FREE online (see NEC reference) | Electrical installation |
| NFPA 70E | Standard for Electrical Safety in the Workplace | FREE online | Electrical safety, arc flash, PPE |
| NFPA 101 | Life Safety Code | FREE online | Means of egress, occupant protection, fire barriers |
| NFPA 1 | Fire Code | FREE online | Fire prevention, hazardous materials |
| NFPA 13 | Standard for the Installation of Sprinkler Systems | FREE online | Automatic sprinkler system design |
| NFPA 13R | Sprinkler Systems in Residential Occupancies | FREE online | Low-rise residential sprinklers |
| NFPA 13D | Sprinkler Systems in One- and Two-Family Dwellings | FREE online | Dwelling sprinklers |
| NFPA 20 | Installation of Stationary Pumps for Fire Protection | FREE online | Fire pump selection and installation |
| NFPA 72 | National Fire Alarm and Signaling Code | FREE online | Fire alarm system design |
| NFPA 110 | Emergency and Standby Power Systems | FREE online | Generator and UPS requirements |
| FM Global Data Sheets | Property loss prevention data sheets | FREE (fmglobal.com) | Insurer-specific requirements, often beyond code minimums |

## Access

- **FREE online view:** NFPA provides free read-only access to ALL its
  standards at https://www.nfpa.org (registration required). This is a
  major advantage over other standards bodies — you can verify section
  numbers and read provisions directly.
- **PDF download:** Requires purchase.
- **No API.**
- **Limitation:** You cannot quote extensively from the free view. Verify
  section numbers via the free view, then cite for the user.

## NFPA 101 — Life Safety Code (key provisions)

| Chapter | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Administration, definitions, classification | Occupancy classification, mixed occupancies |
| 5–7 | Means of Egress | Exit access, exit, exit discharge — the core of life safety |
| 8–11 | Interior finish, fire protection, special structures | Interior flame spread, sprinkler requirements, high-rise |
| 12–23 | Occupancy-specific | Assembly, educational, health care, detention, residential, mercantile, industrial, storage |
| 32–42 | Special structures | High-rise, mall, airport, parking garage |

Key egress parameters (NFPA 101 Chapter 7):
- **Occupant load factor** (Table 7.3.1.2) — sq ft per occupant by occupancy
- **Common path of travel** — maximum before two separate exits required
- **Dead-end corridor** — maximum length
- **Travel distance** — maximum to an exit

## Mandatory-language conventions

- **"shall"** — mandatory
- **"should"** — recommended
- **"may"** — permitted
- **Informative annexes** (Annex A) — explanatory, not enforceable

## Known conflicts and cross-checks

| Conflicts with | Issue |
|---|---|
| IBC | IBC adopts many NFPA standards by reference (NFPA 70, 72, 101). Where both apply, the IBC amendment governs. |
| FM Global | Insurer data sheets often exceed NFPA minimums. Projects insured by FM Global must meet FM data sheet requirements in addition to NFPA. |
| State fire marshal | Local fire marshal interpretation governs enforcement. |
| ASME A17.1 | Elevator safety. NFPA 101 governs egress from elevators; ASME A17.1 governs elevator construction and operation. |

## Jurisdiction notes

- **US (general):** NFPA standards are adopted by reference into state and
  local fire codes. NFPA 101 is widely adopted for life safety; NFPA 13 for
  sprinklers.
- **Health care:** CMS (Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services) requires
  NFPA 101 (2012 edition) and NFPA 99 (Health Care Facilities Code) for
  Medicare-participating facilities.
- **FM Global-insured projects:** FM Data Sheets (free at fmglobal.com) are
  often more restrictive than NFPA and govern when the insurer requires them.

## Free access note

Unlike most engineering standards, NFPA standards are FREE to read online.
Use https://www.nfpa.org/codes-and-standards to verify section numbers
and read provisions directly. This makes NFPA one of the most accessible
US engineering standards for research purposes.
