# ACI 350 — Code Requirements for Environmental Engineering Concrete Structures

## Scope

Concrete structures for environmental engineering: water and wastewater
treatment tanks, clarifiers, digesters, pump stations, and containment
structures. Modifies ACI 318 for the unique exposure conditions of
environmental concrete (chemical attack, saturation, temperature, leak-tight
requirements). Where ACI 350 and ACI 318 differ, ACI 350 governs for
environmental structures.

## Key differences from ACI 318

| Topic | ACI 318 | ACI 350 | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crack control | Standard φ-factor | Tighter crack width limits (0.004–0.010 in) | Leak-tightness for tanks |
| Cover | Standard exposure classes | Increased cover for chemical exposure | Durability in aggressive environments |
| Water-cement ratio | Per exposure class | Lower w/c for sulfate/chemical resistance | Chemical attack resistance |
| Shrinkage/temperature steel | Minimum ratios | Higher minimums for restrained walls | Crack control in saturated conditions |
| Testing | Standard cylinder tests | Additional permeability/leakage tests | Verify leak-tightness |
