Repository

The catalogue

Twenty-five commands, one loop.

Two entry points, five discipline skills, seventeen named research jobs, one application workflow. Everything below runs the same seven-phase method; only the evidence landscape changes.

Discipline and research commands take --deep to force multi-agent coverage or --quick for a fast lookup. --turns N and --budget N set your own ceiling.

Core

Entry points

Start here if you are not sure which skill you want. Both dispatch into the same method.

  • /vitruvius

    The router. Engineering research agent: takes a discipline or a research question and dispatches to the right skill.

  • /engineering-research

    The shared method itself — discover, read, synthesize, verify, review. Takes a research question or an artifact to review.

Disciplines

Evidence landscapes

Each loads a discipline-specific source-routing table and evidence examples into the shared loop. Generated from one payload, so the five cannot drift apart.

  • /mechanical

    Design, thermal, fluids, materials, manufacturing.

  • /software

    Architecture, frameworks, protocols, security, benchmarks.

  • /civil

    Buildings, bridges, steel, concrete, geotech, loads.

  • /electrical

    Power, electronics, controls, signal integrity, EMC.

  • /architectural

    Building science, facades, enclosure, codes, performance.

Workflows

Named engineering jobs

Seventeen skills, each a specific job run over the same loop rather than a parallel method.

  • /gap-analysis

    Identify research gaps via scholarly triangulation across OpenAlex, arXiv and the web.

  • /evidence-ranking

    Rank and score engineering evidence by quality using the tier system.

  • /verifier

    Verify a claim or a calculation against authoritative sources. Blind by default, so the judge does not see the reasoning that produced the claim.

  • /design-alternatives

    Generate and compare three or more engineering approaches with scored trade-off matrices.

  • /fmea-brainstorm

    FMEA-style failure mode brainstorming with severity, occurrence and detection ratings.

  • /hypothesis-generation

    Generate and freeze rival engineering hypotheses with dated evidence boundaries and discriminating tests.

  • /peer-review

    Adversarial peer review of an engineering artifact with severity-graded findings.

  • /compare

    A source-grounded comparison matrix across standards, designs or products.

  • /review

    Severity-graded adversarial review of an artifact, with a concrete revision plan.

  • /audit

    Claim-versus-implementation audit: paper versus code, specification versus design.

  • /summarize

    Faithful structured digest of a standard, a specification or a paper.

  • /eli5

    Plain-language engineering explanation with the jargon removed, not the substance.

  • /artifact-reading

    Anchored extraction from PDFs, drawings and specifications, with page provenance.

  • /scholarly-research

    The academic literature evidence layer: OpenAlex, arXiv, Semantic Scholar, code prior art and page-anchored open-access PDF reading. Synthesis is left to engineering-research.

  • /standards-lookup

    Engineering standards lookup for AISC, ACI, ASCE, IEEE, Eurocode and neighbours, by section.

  • /habit

    Capture durable research preferences from a run. Read-only, abstention-first, and every rule needs explicit approval before it applies.

  • /vitruvius-help

    Quick-reference card for every command and the shared research method.

Application
