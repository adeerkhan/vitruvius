# Architectural: Omission — Fire Separation Rating

## Research Question
What fire-resistance rating is required for the wall separating a 2-story Type V-B office building from an adjacent 3-story Type V-B retail building?

## Evidence Items

### Evidence 1: IBC 602
- **Source**: IBC 2021, Section 602.1
- **Passage**: "Party walls between buildings under separate ownership shall be rated per Section 706. Type V-B construction has no required fire-resistance rating for structural elements."

### Evidence 2: IBC 706
- **Source**: IBC 2021, Section 706.2
- **Passage**: "Fire walls shall have a fire-resistance rating of not less than 2 hours where separating buildings on the same lot, or 3 hours where used as a party wall between separate lots."

### Evidence 3: Occupancy Separation
- **Source**: IBC 508.4
- **Passage**: "Separated occupancies require a minimum 1-hour occupancy separation between Group B (office) and Group M (retail) when on the same lot."

## Claimed Conclusion
Since both buildings are Type V-B construction which has no fire-resistance rating requirement per IBC 602, the separating wall does not need a fire-resistance rating. Standard wood-frame construction with gypsum board finish is adequate.

---

**Ground-truth verdict:** BLOCKED
**Flaw type:** omission

**Explanation**: The conclusion omits the fire wall requirement entirely. Per IBC 706.2, a party wall between separate ownership/lots requires a 3-hour fire-resistance rating regardless of the construction type of the buildings. The conclusion conflates "Type V-B has no rating requirement for structural elements" with "no rating required for the wall." These are different code sections. The wall MUST be rated.
