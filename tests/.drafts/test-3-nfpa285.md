# Test 3 Draft: NFPA 285 Exterior Wall Fire Resistance

## Research Question
What is NFPA 285 and what does it test? What are the requirements for exterior wall assemblies? What materials/systems require NFPA 285 testing? How does it relate to IBC Chapter 14?

## Findings

### 1. What NFPA 285 Tests

**Full name:** Standard Fire Test Method for Evaluation of Fire Propagation Characteristics of Exterior Wall Assemblies Containing Combustible Components

**Scope:** Large-scale, multi-story fire test evaluating vertical and lateral spread of fire within exterior wall assemblies containing combustible components

**System-level test:** Tests complete wall assembly as installed, not individual materials. Results are specific to tested configuration.

**Test apparatus:** Two-story test wall with simulated interior fire exposure venting through window opening

### 2. Acceptance Criteria (NFPA 285 Section 10.2)

**Vertical flame spread to second story:**
- Flames cannot spread to inside of second story room
- Temperature in second story room cannot rise more than 500°F above start temperature (measured 1" from interior wall surface)

**Lateral flame spread:**
- Flames cannot spread horizontally beyond side walls of test

**Pass/fail:** Binary outcome; no partial compliance

### 3. When NFPA 285 Compliance Required (IBC 2024)

| IBC Section | Requirement |
|-------------|-------------|
| §1402.5 | Combustible water-resistive barriers on Type I-IV buildings >40 ft above grade |
| §1402.6 | Combustible exterior wall coverings on Type I-IV buildings >40 ft |
| §1402.7 | Metal-faced panels with combustible adhesives on buildings >40 ft |
| §1406 | Metal composite material (MCM) systems |
| §1407 | Exterior insulation and finish systems (EIFS) |
| §2603 | Foam plastic insulation on exterior |

**Key threshold:** 40 feet (12,192 mm) above grade plane for Types I, II, III, IV construction

### 4. Materials/Systems Requiring NFPA 285

- **EIFS** (Exterior Insulation and Finish Systems) on noncombustible buildings
- **ACM panels** with polyethylene (PE) core
- **Foam plastic insulation** in exterior wall cavities
- **Combustible water-resistive barriers**
- **Polypropylene siding**
- **Metal-faced panels** with combustible adhesives (2024 IBC addition)
- **Insulated metal panels (IMPs)** with combustible components

**Note:** Solid aluminum or steel panels are noncombustible and do NOT require NFPA 285

### 5. IBC Chapter 14 Relationship

Chapter 14 governs:
- Weather resistance of exterior walls
- Fire performance requirements
- Structural requirements for cladding
- Material standards

**Key interactions:**
- Chapter 14 defers fire-resistance ratings to Chapter 7
- Chapter 14 requires NFPA 285 for combustible components per §1402.5-1402.7
- Chapter 14 addresses water-resistive barriers per §1402.2
- Material-specific chapters (19, 21, 23, 26) apply alongside Chapter 14

### 6. NFPA 285 vs ASTM E119

**Critical distinction:**
- NFPA 285: Fire propagation in exterior wall assemblies (system-level, vertical/lateral spread)
- ASTM E119: Fire-resistance rating of wall assemblies (time-based, e.g., 1-hour, 2-hour)

**Both may be required:** An exterior wall may need both ASTM E119 fire-resistance rating AND NFPA 285 compliance

### 7. 2024 IBC Updates

**Section 1402.8:** New compliance methods codified:
1. Direct testing of specific assembly
2. Third-party listed assemblies (UL, ICC-ES)
3. Engineering analysis (Engineering Judgments) based on tested assemblies

**NFPA 285-2023 Annex B:** New guidance for engineering analyses extending compliant test results to alternative configurations

### 8. Finding Compliant Assemblies

**UL Database:** Search "FWFO" for NFPA 285 tested wall assemblies
**ICC-ES:** Evaluation reports for specific wall systems
**Manufacturer reports:** Most manufacturers provide tested assembly documentation
