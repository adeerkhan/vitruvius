# Test 2 Draft: ADA Accessibility Door Requirements

## Research Question
What are the ADA/ABA accessibility requirements for doors? What are the clear width, force, and threshold requirements? What are the exceptions?

## Findings

### 1. Clear Width Requirements (§404.2.3)

- **Minimum:** 32 inches (815 mm) clear opening width
- **Measurement:** Between face of door and stop on latch side when door is open 90°
- **Projection rule:** Projections between 34" and 80" above floor cannot exceed 4" into clear opening
- **Practical note:** Standard 36" (3'0") door typically provides ~34" clear width, meeting requirement
- **Nominal 32" doors:** Generally do NOT provide full 32" clear after accounting for door thickness and hinge geometry

### 2. Opening Force (§404.2.9)

- **Interior hinged doors:** 5 pounds-force (lbf) maximum
- **Fire-rated doors:** Exempt from 5 lbf limit; defer to minimum force required by fire code
- **Exterior doors:** No maximum specified (impacted by wind loading, gasketing, HVAC, door weight)
- **Recommendation:** Automation recommended for exterior doors with significant opening force

### 3. Threshold Requirements (§404.2.5)

- **Maximum height:** 1/2 inch (12.7 mm)
- **Bevel requirement:** If threshold exceeds 1/4" (6.4 mm), bevel with slope no steeper than 1:2
- **At/below 1/4":** No beveling required

### 4. Closing Speed (§404.2.8)

- **Standard closers:** 5 seconds minimum from 90° to 12° from latch
- **Spring hinges:** 1.5 seconds minimum from 70° to 0°

### 5. Hardware Requirements (§404.2.7)

- Operable with one hand
- No tight grasping, pinching, or twisting of wrist
- Operable with no more than 5 lbf force
- **Prohibited:** Round doorknobs (require twisting motion)
- **Compliant types:** Lever handles, push/pull bars, panic exit devices

### 6. Smooth Surface (§404.2.10)

- Push side of swinging doors
- Bottom 10 inches above finished floor
- Full width of door
- Purpose: Allows wheelchair footrest to push door without catching

### 7. Maneuvering Clearance (§404.2.4)

- Required on both sides of door
- Exception: Not required on both sides if door is used in one direction only
- Floor must be level (max 1:48 slope)
- Dimensions vary by door type, approach direction, and presence of closer

### 8. Vision Lights (§404.2.11)

- Bottom of vision light at 43" maximum above floor
- Exception: Not required if lowest part is above 66" from floor

### 9. Exceptions

- **§404.1 Ex:** Doors operated only by security personnel exempt from hardware, closing speed, opening force requirements (but must meet other requirements including maneuvering clearance)
- **Fire-rated doors:** Opening force governed by fire code, not ADA 5 lbf limit
- **Hospital patient room doors:** Exception for maneuvering clearance (44" width for gurneys per original ADAAG)
