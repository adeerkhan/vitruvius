# Test 6 Plan: Green Building — LEED v4.1 Energy & Atmosphere

## Research Question
What are the LEED v4.1 prerequisites and credits for the Building Energy and Atmosphere category? What is the Energy Cost Budget (ECB) method vs Performance Rating Method? What are the points available? How does ASHRAE 90.1-2019 relate to LEED energy credits?

- LEED v4.1 Online Rating System Tool (usgbc.org)

- EPA ENERGY STAR Portfolio Manager
- USGBC LEED credit library

## Evidence to Gather
1. EA Prerequisite: Minimum Energy Performance — what it requires
2. EA Credit: Optimize Energy Performance — points available, thresholds
3. ECB method vs Performance Rating Method — definitions, when each applies
4. ASHRAE 90.1-2019 baseline requirements
5. Whole-building energy modeling requirements
6. Commissioning prerequisite (EA Prerequisite: Fundamental Commissioning)
7. Additional EA credits (Advanced Energy, Enhanced Commissioning, Renewable Energy)

## Verification Criteria
- LEED v4.1 prerequisites and credits correctly named
- Point ranges match current LEED v4.1 BD+C
- ECB and Performance Rating Method defined per ASHRAE 90.1
- ASHRAE 90.1-2019 edition correctly referenced (not 90.1-2016)
- Current LEED version confirmed
