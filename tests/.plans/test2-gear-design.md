# Test 2: Gear Design — AGMA Stress Numbers

## Research Question
What are the AGMA bending stress and contact stress numbers for a typical steel spur gear pair? What factors (Ka, Km, Kv) modify these? Cite AGMA 2001 or equivalent.

## Plan

### Phase 1: Source Discovery
- Search for ANSI/AGMA 2001-D04 standard
- Locate bending stress formula (pitting resistance)
- Locate contact stress formula (bending strength)
- Document K-factors and their definitions

### Phase 2: Formula Extraction
- Extract bending stress formula: σ = Wt·Ko·Kv·Ks·Pd/(F·Km·KB/J)
- Extract contact stress formula: σc = Cp·√(Wt·Ko·Kv·Ks·Km·Cf/(d·F·I))
- Document all modification factors and their typical ranges

### Phase 3: Factor Analysis
- Overload factor Ko: 1.0–1.75
- Dynamic factor Kv: depends on gear quality and velocity
- Size factor Ks: typically 1.0
- Load distribution factor Km: 1.0–1.6
- Elastic coefficient Cp: material-dependent
