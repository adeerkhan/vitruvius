## Research Questions

## Standards to Check

- IEEE C57.12.00 — General Requirements for Liquid-Immersed Transformers

- IEEE C57.12.91 — Standard Test Code for Dry-Type Transformers
- IEEE C57.110 — Recommended Practice for Transformers Serving Nonlinear Loads
- ANSI/NETA MTS — Standard for Maintenance Testing
- NEC Article 450 — Transformers

## Evidence to Gather

- Sizing methodology (kVA demand vs connected load)
- Transformer losses: no-load (P_NO) and load (P_L) losses
- Efficiency formula: η = P_out / (P_out + P_NO + P_L × (load factor)²)
- K-factor definition and calculation (IEEE C57.110)
- K-factor transformer ratings (K-4, K-13, K-20)
- Impedance requirements (%Z typical: 2-6%)
- Temperature rise ratings (65°C, 80°C, 115°C)
- Selection criteria for dry-type vs liquid-filled
- Typical impedance by kVA rating
