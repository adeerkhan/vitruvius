## Blind Verifier Protocol (7 checks)
1. Source authenticity — are standards real?
2. Citation specificity — article/section numbers present?
3. Numerical accuracy — do calculations check out?
4. Completeness — key facts missing?
5. Consistency — do sources agree?
6. Practical relevance — applicable in field?
7. Limitations acknowledged — what's NOT covered?
