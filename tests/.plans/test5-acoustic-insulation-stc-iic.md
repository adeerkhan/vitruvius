# Test 5 Plan: Acoustic Insulation — STC and IIC Ratings

## Research Question
What are STC (Sound Transmission Class) and IIC (Impact Insulation Class) ratings? How are they measured (ASTM E90, ASTM E492)? What are the IBC/IRC minimum requirements for wall and floor assemblies? What are typical STC values for common wall assemblies? How does flanking transmission affect performance?

## Sources to Search
- ASTM E90: Standard Test Method for Laboratory Measurement of Airborne Sound Transmission
- ASTM E492: Standard Test Method for Laboratory Measurement of Impact Sound Transmission
- IBC 2024: Chapter 12 (Interior Environment) / Table 1207.2
- IRC 2021: Table R302.3.1
- Gypsum Association GA-416
- NRC (National Research Council Canada) acoustic guidelines

## Evidence to Gather
1. STC definition, measurement method (ASTM E90), frequency range
2. IIC definition, measurement method (ASTM E492), tapping machine
3. IBC/IRC minimum STC/IIC values for walls and floors
4. Typical STC values for common assemblies (stud + drywall, etc.)
5. Flanking transmission: definition, mechanism, code treatment
6. Field vs lab performance differences

## Verification Criteria
- ASTM test method numbers correct
- IBC/IRC table references verifiable
- STC/IIC rating scale ranges plausible (0-100)
- Flanking transmission concept accurately described
- Current code editions referenced
