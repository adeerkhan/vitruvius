# Peer review: brief not yet supplied

You asked for a peer review of your research brief before submission. No brief
came with the request — no path, text, link, or attachment — so there is
nothing yet to review. I won't invent its claims, numbers, or citations to
review in their place.

## What I need from you

Send whatever you have; one message is enough:

- **The brief** — file path, pasted text, or URL/PDF.
- **The question and decision** it answers (what changes if it is wrong).
- **Governing standard or code**, with edition and jurisdiction, if any applies.
- **Backing material** — calculations, datasets, scripts, or drawings behind any
  number in it.
- **Effort budget** — a fast pass or a thorough review, and your deadline.

## What the review will apply

1. **Claim inventory** — every load-bearing number, unit, citation, and
   conclusion, listed before judgment.
2. **Source verification** — trace each critical claim to a standard section,
   URL, or artifact; re-derive calculations from the stated formula, inputs, and
   units; check the cited source is the right domain, edition, and jurisdiction.
3. **Strength sweeps** — numerical rigor (data independence, uncertainty),
   internal consistency (summary matches body, conclusions match results),
   and reproducibility (could a reader rebuild the result from what is stated?).
4. **Severity-graded findings** — FATAL (unsupported or non-compliant, fix
   before use), MAJOR (likely wrong or a real gap), MINOR (clarity and
   consistency). Each finding is pinned to a section or line with the evidence
   that supports it; unclear or unreadable sources are marked **blocked**, never
   passed. Empty tiers are reported as `none`, not padded.
5. **Revision plan** — fixes ordered by severity.

## What you get back

A short summary with FATAL/MAJOR/MINOR counts and the top three issues with
evidence trails, plus the full review and its provenance sidecar (sources
consulted, accepted, rejected) saved under `outputs/peer-review/`.

I review independently and report findings — I do not rewrite the brief, and I
do not sign off on it. Research-only; final engineering sign-off needs a
licensed engineer.

Send the document and I will start with the claim inventory.
