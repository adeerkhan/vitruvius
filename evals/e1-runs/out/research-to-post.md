# Twitter thread — blocked on the paper

**No paper came through with your message.** I don't have a PDF, link, arXiv/DOI, repo, or attached text, so I can't see the result you want the thread about.

I won't draft around a guessed result. A thread that misstates what the paper found is worse than no thread, and this skill reads the source before writing.

**Send me one of these and I'll draft immediately:**

- a PDF path or attachment
- an arXiv ID, DOI, or URL
- the abstract pasted in, plus the headline number if you have it
- "own idea" — then tell me the claim and I'll write from that, not from a paper

**Optional, but it makes the hooks better:** one line on your own reaction. "I didn't expect the error to drop that fast" or "this contradicts what I assumed." I'll quote it, not rewrite it. If you have none, say so and I'll continue.

**What you'll get back:**

- 2–3 short posts, plain language, no jargon dump
- Post 1 opens the question, no link; last post carries the source link with 📰
- One best image from the paper, if it has a usable figure
- A copy-first card at `outputs/<slug>-post.html`, with a Copy button per post
- The markdown draft with the source, your quoted thought, the chosen angle, and the posts kept separate

Before writing I'll offer 2–3 candidate angles, each tied to a line in the paper, and you pick one. If none fit, say "no real connection" and I'll write a question post instead.

**Boundaries:** this is a draft only. Nothing gets posted, scheduled, or scraped from X. The source link is the provenance — no claim ledger, no verification section.

One question, then I'll proceed: **which paper, and where can I read it?**
