# Wind Loading on Long-Span Bridges

**Governing provisions.** In the US, bridge wind loads come from AASHTO LRFD Bridge Design Specifications Art. 3.8, not ASCE 7 (which addresses buildings and other structures, not highway bridges). Design uses a 3-second gust at 33 ft in open terrain with 7% probability of exceedance in 50 years (≈700-year return period); Strength III takes the site value from Fig. 3.8.1.1.2-1, Strength V is 80 mph, Service I 70 mph, Service IV 0.75 × Strength III. Eurocode EN 1991-1-4 §8 covers bridges only up to 200 m span (§1.1(2)); longer spans require site-specific study.

**Static load.** PZ = 2.56 × 10⁻⁶ V² KZ G CD (ksf, V in mph) on the projected area, with KZ for exposure and elevation, G the gust factor, CD the drag coefficient (1.3 for box and I-girder sections, higher for trusses). Wind on live load is 0.10 klf, 6 ft above the deck (3.8.1.3).

**Why span drives the problem.** AASHTO 3.8.3.1 deems a bridge wind-sensitive when span/width or span/depth exceeds 30. Art. 3.8.3.1.2 requires buffeting, vortex excitation, galloping, flutter and static divergence to be considered; 3.8.3.3 requires freedom from divergence or catastrophic flutter below 1.2 × design wind speed, and from fatigue caused by vortex-induced or galloping oscillation.

**Mechanisms.** Vortex shedding and lock-in (serviceability, low speed — Great Belt before opening; Volgograd, 2010); torsional flutter, self-excited and divergent (Tacoma Narrows, 1940: 853 m span, ≈19 m/s); buffeting from turbulence; galloping; rain-wind cable vibration at 7–15 m/s, driven by a moving water rivulet (Hikami & Shiraishi, Meiko-Nishi Bridge, 1988).

**Method.** Davenport aerodynamic admittance with Scanlan flutter derivatives; spring-mounted section models for derivatives and critical speed; full aeroelastic models for three-dimensional and erection-stage response (Akashi Kaikyo, 1991 m, 1:100 model, girder design speed 60 m/s). Countermeasures: streamlined box with fairings or stabilizer, helical fillets, dampers, cross-ties.

**Sources.** AASHTO LRFD, 9th ed., Art. 3.8.1.1.2, 3.8.1.2.1, 3.8.1.3, 3.8.3; EN 1991-1-4:2005 §1.1(2), §8; FHWA-HRT-05-084; Fujino & Siringoringo, IABSE SEI 23(3), 2013.
