# Pressure Vessel Closure — Three Design Alternatives

**No closure artifact was supplied.** No drawing, diameter, design pressure/temperature, fluid, duty cycle, or jurisdiction was given. The comparison below is an illustrative screening under stated assumptions, not a design.

**Assumptions (change these and the ranking moves):** 1.0 m ID; 1.0 MPa(g) at 100 °C; non-lethal, non-toxic service; carbon steel; ASME BPVC Section VIII Div. 1, 2023 edition; infrequent access unless noted.

| | A. Bolted flat cover | B. Bolted domed cover | C. Quick-actuating closure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Approach | Flat blind + confined spiral-wound gasket (UG-34; Mandatory App. 2) | 2:1 ellipsoidal cover welded to a bolted flange ring (UG-32 + App. 2) | Split-ring/clamp closure with safety interlock (UG-35, Other Types of Closures) |
| Advantages | Lowest cost; simple NDE; easy rework | Membrane action cuts weight/thickness; good leak margin | Fast, tool-free access |
| Limitations | Heavy; bending governs; gasket seating sensitive | Higher fabrication and weld cost | Mechanism, interlock, and qualification burden; most failure modes |

**Weighted comparison (1–5, 5 = best; infrequent access).** Weights: pressure/weight 25%, first cost 20%, leak integrity 20%, fabrication 15%, access 10%, safety/qualification 10%.

| Option | Score |
|---|---|
| A flat | 3.35 |
| B domed | 3.45 |
| C quick | 2.95 |

**Ranking and evidence.** For infrequent access, B first: dished heads carry pressure in membrane rather than bending (UG-32 versus UG-34), so the weight and leak advantage is structural, not preference. A is the budget/low-pressure choice. C ranks last here because UG-35 adds interlock and mechanism checks that only pay back with frequent access. Re-weighting access to 30% reverses the order: C 3.45, B 3.15, A 3.05 — if the vessel opens daily, C is the rational choice.

Scores are analyst judgments anchored to code roles, not measured values; A and B scores need the missing pressure and diameter to become quantitative. Code paragraph titles were checked for the 2023 edition (UG-32, UG-34, UG-35); Appendix 2 is cited by title only, not opened.
