---
description: Architectural research (building science, facades, codes, performance, precedents). Usage: /architectural <research question>
---

Run the architectural research skill on the $ARGUMENTS research question:
activate `/skill:architectural`, which dispatches to the shared
engineering-research method with the architectural evidence landscape (IBC,
ADA, ASHRAE, ASTM; code text and commentary; evaluation reports) and
verification criteria (code section + edition, named assemblies, test-method
traceability for performance values). Follow the research loop in
/skill:engineering-research and produce a provenance sidecar. Never fabricate
a source.
