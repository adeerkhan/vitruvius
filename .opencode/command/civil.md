description: Civil / structural engineering research (buildings, bridges, steel, concrete, geotech, loads). Usage: /civil <research question>
---

Run the civil and structural engineering research skill on the $ARGUMENTS
research question: activate `/skill:civil`, which dispatches to the shared
engineering-research method with the civil evidence landscape (AISC, ACI,
ASCE 7, IBC, AASHTO; code commentary) and verification criteria (code section
+ edition, load basis, LRFD/SFD factors, material grade + spec). Follow the
research loop in /skill:engineering-research and produce a provenance
sidecar. Never fabricate a source.
