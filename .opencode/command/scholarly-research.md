---
description: Academic literature discovery for engineering research (OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, arXiv, alphaXiv). Usage: /scholarly-research <topic or paper identifier>
---

Run the scholarly research skill on the $ARGUMENTS topic: activate
`/skill:scholarly-research`. Discover papers via keyless REST (OpenAlex
primary, then Semantic Scholar, arXiv, alphaXiv fast search), then the host's
web/browser tools for non-academic sources. Do not scrape Google Scholar. For
every claim record title, authors, year, venue, DOI/arXiv id, and the URL
fetched. Mark full-text access blocked when paywalled. Write findings as an
evidence table with stable numeric IDs.
